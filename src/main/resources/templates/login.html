Privacy Policy

Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy outlines the types of information we collect and how it is used.

1. Data Collection

When you log in via Google OAuth, we collect basic profile information: your Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture (Avatar). We also securely store an access/refresh token to interact with your calendar.

2. Calendar Data Usage

SmartBooking fetches your upcoming calendar events strictly to calculate your free/busy availability. We do not store the contents, titles, or descriptions of your private calendar events in our database. We only store bookings created directly through our platform.

3. Data Security

All sensitive information, including email addresses of participants, is encrypted in our database using standard algorithms. Access to the backend system is strictly limited to authorized university administrators.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. Your data is used exclusively for the operation of the SmartBooking platform within Harokopio University.
