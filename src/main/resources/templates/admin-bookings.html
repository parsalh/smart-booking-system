Help & FAQ

The time slot suggestions don't reflect my real availability

Availability is checked based on how your events show up on Google Calendar. If an event is set to "Free" instead of "Busy", SmartBooking will treat you as available during that time. Make sure your important events have "Busy" enabled on Google Calendar.

What does "Out of Office" do on my profile?

While an Out of Office period is active, SmartBooking treats you as unavailable for the entire duration when suggesting meeting times, useful for vacations or periods when you don't want meetings scheduled on your behalf.

I invited someone without a SmartBooking account

They'll receive a sign-up invitation email, and separately, a normal Google Calendar invite once the meeting is confirmed, no account is needed to respond via Google Calendar. If the sign-up email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, ask them to check their spam or junk folder.

How does responding (RSVP) to an invite work?

You can respond either from within SmartBooking or directly from Gmail/Google Calendar, your response syncs automatically in both directions.

I cancelled a meeting directly on Google Calendar, why does it still show up?

Give it a moment until your calendar next reloads, the system periodically checks whether bookings are still active on Google Calendar and updates accordingly.

I got a Google security alert about book-a-meeting.site accessing my account

This is expected and not a security concern. Google automatically sends this notification whenever you grant a new app access to your account through Google Sign-In, exactly what happens the first time you log in to SmartBooking with your Google account. It confirms that SmartBooking can read and manage your Calendar on your behalf, the same permission you approved during sign-in. You can review or revoke this access anytime from your Google Account's third-party access settings.
